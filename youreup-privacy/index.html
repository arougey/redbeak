Privacy Policy — You're Up

Last updated: 21 November 2026

You're Up is a family game you play in person, passing one phone around a room. It is built to need as little about you as possible.

What stays on your device

Everything you create in the app is stored only on your phone, in the app's own storage. None of it is sent anywhere, and we cannot see any of it:

  • the names you add to the roster
  • who has won which games
  • how many rounds you have played
  • the packs you have switched on, and any cards you have written yourself
  • your settings, such as whether sound is on

Deleting the app deletes all of it permanently. There is no account, no backup and no way to restore it, because there is nowhere for it to have been kept.

What we do not do

You're Up has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party trackers. It does not ask for your contacts, your camera, your microphone, your photos or your location. The only sensor it uses is motion, so Forehead guess can tell when you tilt the phone; those readings never leave the device. It does not build a profile of you, and there is nothing about you for us to sell — we could not share your data with anyone even if we wanted to, because we never receive it.

Purchases

If you buy a pass or a subscription, the payment is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your card details, billing address or any other payment information.

To remember what you have bought — including on a new phone when you tap "Restore purchases" — the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase-management service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier generated for your install and the record of which products you purchased. It does not receive your name, your email address or anything you have typed into the app. RevenueCat's own privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

App updates

To fix bugs and add cards without a full store update, the app checks for updates from Expo, the service it is built and delivered with, at u.expo.dev. Each check sends your device's platform (iOS or Android), the app's version and release channel, which update is currently installed, and a random identifier generated for your install so an update can be rolled out gradually. As with any connection, Expo also sees your IP address. Nothing you have entered in the app is sent. Expo's privacy policy is at expo.dev/privacy.

Children

You're Up is made for families and groups of all ages, and contains no alcohol, adult themes or ads. It does not collect personal information from anyone, children included: nothing you type into the app leaves your phone, and the only data that does (an anonymous install identifier, purchase records and the update check) is described above. Purchases always go through the App Store or Google Play, with whatever parental controls are set on the phone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. Any change that affects what leaves your device will also be called out in the app's release notes.
