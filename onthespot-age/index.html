Age Suitability — On The Spot

Last updated: 11 October 2026

18+

On The Spot is made for adults and is rated 18+ on the App Store and for mature audiences on Google Play. It is a party game for groups playing together in the same room, passing one phone around.

Why it is rated 18+

The app has two kinds of games. The Games shelf (Charades, Forehead guess, Spy, Imposter word, Word chain, Word bomb) is mostly guessing and word play. The Party shelf deals question, dare and "never have I ever" cards in which a player who loses takes a "punishment" agreed by the group, which at many parties means a drink. Across the app you will find:

  • Alcohol — frequent references, and party games played with drinks in mind. There are also occasional references to drugs and vaping.
  • Sexual themes — questions and dares about dating, relationships and sex, written in text. There are no images of sexual content or nudity.
  • Mature themes and mild language — adult situations and a small amount of crude language.

Ratings apply to everything the app can show, including optional and paid content, which is why the whole app is 18+ even though most cards are mild.

What is switched off by default

The most explicit modes, Turning up the heat and Scorched, never appear unless someone turns on the Explicit content switch shown at the top of the app. It starts off on every new install. Starting a game with those modes switched on asks for a second confirmation first. They are also part of the paid upgrade, so they are not available on the free tier at all.

What the app does not have

  • No chat, messaging or online play — nobody outside the room can contact a player.
  • No sharing of anything players write; custom cards stay on the phone.
  • No accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • No gambling with money. Some cards ask the group to "bet" on an outcome, but the only stakes are the game's punishments.
  • No location, camera, microphone or contacts access. The only sensor used is motion, to detect tilts in Forehead guess.

Playing responsibly

Every card is a suggestion. Nobody has to drink, do a dare or answer a question, and any player can pass. If your group drinks, drink responsibly and within the law, and never drink and drive. Our Terms of Use say more.

For parents and guardians

On The Spot is not intended for anyone under 18. You can stop it being downloaded on a child's device with the parental controls built into each platform:

  • iPhone and iPad — Settings › Screen Time › Content & Privacy Restrictions › App Store, Media, Web & Games › Apps, and choose a rating below 18+.
  • Android — Google Family Link, or Play Store › Settings › Family › Parental controls, and set the app rating limit below Mature.

The app collects nothing about the people who use it; see the Privacy Policy.
