Frequently asked

I bought a pack and it is not there.
Open Settings from the first screen and tap Restore purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, so this also brings them back on a new phone.

Does a pack work in every game?
Across its whole shelf, yes. Buying a pack on the Party side unlocks it in every party game, and the same on the Games side. The cards are written separately for each game, so the same pack reads differently depending on what you are playing.

How do I cancel a subscription?
In your device Settings rather than in the app — open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Apple handles it and we never see your payment details.

I lost my players and stats.
Everything lives only on your phone, so deleting the app deletes it for good. There is no account and no backup, which is also why we never see any of it.
