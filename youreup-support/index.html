Frequently asked

I bought a pass or subscription and the packs are still locked.
Open Settings from the first screen and tap Restore purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account or Google Account, so this also brings them back on a new phone.

Does a card pack work in every game?
Yes. Switching a pack on adds its cards to every game, written separately for each one, so the same pack plays differently depending on the game.

How do I cancel a subscription?
In your store account rather than in the app. On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. On Android, open the Google Play app, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions. Apple and Google handle it, and we never see your payment details.

I lost my players and stats.
Everything lives only on your phone, so deleting the app deletes it for good. There is no account and no backup, which is also why we never see any of it.
