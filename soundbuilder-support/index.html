Frequently asked

How do I restore my Premium purchase?
Open the app, go to the Profile tab, and tap Restore Purchases.

Audio stops when I lock my phone.
Start playback, then lock the screen — sound should continue with media controls in the notification / lock screen. If it doesn't, make sure background audio is allowed for SoundBuilder in your device settings.

How do I share a build?
Open a saved build and tap the share icon to send a link to friends.
